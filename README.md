##  Slot Machine Assignment Game
Play the game directly in your browser without any installations

## How to Play

Each player is given 50 coins to start.
# 3 cherry's gives 100 coins.
# 3 bell's gives 200 coins.
# 3 bar's gives 500 coins.
# 3 7's gives 1000 coins.

1. Click the live link below to open the game in your browser:
  https://kur0m1i1.github.io/Slot-Machine-Assignment/ 
2. Wait a few moments for the game assembly to finish loading.
3. Click anywhere inside the game window once it loads to focus your mouse and keyboard controls.

---
## *Game Controls*

1. Click on the lever with the red ball to start the rolling!!
2. Click on the Bet Options Panel on the right to manage your bets.
3. Reload the link to reset the coins.

---

## System Requirements & Troubleshooting

* **Supported Browsers:** Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Apple Safari.
* **Mobile Warning:** This game is optimized for desktop computers. It may not run correctly or support touch controls on smartphones and tablets.
* **Stuck on a Blank Screen?** If the game fails to load, try hard-refreshing your browser (`Ctrl + F5` on Windows or `Cmd + Shift + R` on Mac) or open the link in an Incognito / Private window.
